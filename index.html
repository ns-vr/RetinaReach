01 / Quality is the first gate

A result is only as useful as the image behind it.

Blur, poor lighting, low contrast, and a cropped retinal field can weaken any downstream analysis. RetinaReach puts image quality at the start of the workflow, not in the fine print.

Explore the states to see how the interface communicates next steps. This is an illustrative interaction, not a validated clinical quality classifier.

02 / A deliberate path

Less black box.
More clear next steps.

Every screening should leave a readable trail: what was checked, what remains uncertain, and what needs a human decision.

STEP 01

Capture

Record a participant code, eye, date, and the image being reviewed.

STEP 02

Check quality

Inspect basic image properties and flag issues that may need a recapture.

STEP 03

Review responsibly

Keep model output separate from quality checks. Route uncertain cases for review.

STEP 04

Keep a record

Save a local screening summary for the prototype workflow and export a CSV when needed.

04 / The responsibility layer

Useful technology knows what it cannot do.

RetinaReach is a prototype for screening workflow design. It is not a medical device, and it does not diagnose diabetic retinopathy.

✓
No invented diagnosesNo model prediction is shown unless a real model is connected.
✓
Clear demo boundariesImage checks are heuristic demonstrations, not clinically validated thresholds.
✓
Human review mattersReal screening decisions require appropriate clinical oversight.
✓
